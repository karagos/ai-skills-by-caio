# Assets

Drop two files here to personalize your carousels:

- `logo.svg` (or `logo.png`): your brand logo. Transparent background preferred.
- `headshot.png`: your profile photo. Square crop, 500x500 pixels minimum.

If `headshot.png` is missing, the CTA slide will fall back to your initials (derived from `owner_name` in `brand-config.yaml`) on a colored circle.

If `logo.svg` is missing, the logo placement stays blank. Templates degrade gracefully either way.

Supported formats: SVG or PNG for logo. PNG or JPG for headshot.
